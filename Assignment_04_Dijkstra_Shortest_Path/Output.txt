SMART CITY EMERGENCY ROUTE SYSTEM
Intersections: 0 to 5; ambulance starts at node 0
Hospitals are located at nodes 4 and 5.

Before traffic update:
Route: 0 -> 2 -> 1 -> 3 -> 4
Travel time: 10 minutes
Nearest hospital: 4

Traffic update: road 2 <-> 1 changes from 1 to 12 minutes.

After traffic update:
Route: 0 -> 1 -> 3 -> 4
Travel time: 11 minutes
Nearest hospital: 4

Algorithm: Dijkstra's shortest path
Time complexity: O((V + E) log V) with a priority queue
